## Rack 2 is lit from the bin-7 end

- **Rack 2's strips start at bin 7.** Each row of rack 2 is now fed from its bin-7 end, so A7, B7 … G7 are the first LED of their strands and bin 1 is at the far end. Rack 1 is unchanged: every row still starts at bin 1.
- **New ESPHome substitution `din_column`.** It says which end of the rows the data line enters: `"1"` (rack 1) or the rack's last column (`"7"` on rack 2). Everything else in the config counts from that end.

**Re-flash rack 2** with the new `examples/esphome/winerack2led.yaml` (or add `din_column: "7"` and the one new line in `bin_first_led` to your copy). Rack 1's boards need nothing.
