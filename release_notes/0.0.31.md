## Rack map on the dashboard

- **See the racks bin by bin.** The new grid button beside the light bulb shows each LED rack as its grid of bins, every bin in the colour its LEDs show. Tap a bin to see its bottles and light it on the rack. An empty bin lights too, to find a space for a bottle you are putting away.
- **The map follows your filters.** With a chip or a search on, only the bottles in the list colour their bins, matching what the racks show.

Nothing to re-flash: the boards are unchanged. If the dashboard still looks the same after updating, bump the `?v=` on your iframe card URL.
