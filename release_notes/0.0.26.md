## Racks that go dark when you leave

- **New option: Turn the racks off after (seconds).** Under Settings → Devices & Services → CellarTracker → Configure. Set it to, say, `120`, and the LED racks are dark until something asks for them. They go dark again two minutes after the last thing you did on the dashboard. Each tap, filter or search starts the two minutes again, and a bottle you located stays lit until then. `0`, the default, keeps the overview lit all the time, as before.
- **Light the racks when someone comes in.** A new optional blueprint, *CellarTracker: light the racks when someone comes in*, takes your cellar's door contacts or motion sensors. When one turns on and the racks are dark, it shows the overview. If the dashboard is already showing something, it keeps that lit longer instead. Import it from the README. You need only one, for all the racks.
- **Syncs no longer wipe a highlight.** A CellarTracker sync, a changed grid or a board coming back now repaints only over the overview. It never lights a rack that went dark, and never replaces the bottles you filtered for.
- `cellar_tracker.show_overview` takes `only_if_dark`, and the grid sensors carry a `showing` attribute (`overview`, `highlight` or `off`).

**Re-import *paint a wine rack*** (⋮ → Re-import blueprint) to get the new behaviour. The integration update alone isn't enough.
