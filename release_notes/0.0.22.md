## The dashboard lights your wine racks

If you have LED strips behind your racks, the dashboard now drives them:

- **Tap a bottle** and its bin lights on its own, with the rest of the rack dark. The drawer also has a **Show on rack** button.
- **Tap a chip or search**, and the racks show only the bottles in the list, in their drinking-window colours. For example, *Ready to drink* lights exactly what you can open tonight.
- Go back to **All wines** with an empty search and the overview returns. A highlight also gives way to the overview by itself after ten minutes.
- A **Light racks** switch next to the sort control turns this off on that device. It appears only once LED racks are set up.

Behind it are three new actions, `cellar_tracker.locate_bottle`, `cellar_tracker.highlight_bottles` and `cellar_tracker.show_overview`, which also work from automations. They don't talk to the boards directly. Each one fires an event naming the rack, and that rack's automation does the lighting.

## The rack automations are now blueprints

The old `wine_rack_leds.yaml` package has been replaced by two blueprints that import with one click from the README:

- **Paint a wine rack** (automation) keeps a rack's LEDs showing the cellar, and answers the dashboard.
- **Wine rack controls** (script) lights one bin, shows the overview, or turns the rack off.

Create one of each per rack. The sensors are picked from dropdowns, so there are no entity IDs to edit.

**Coming from the package?** Delete `<config>/packages/wine_rack_leds.yaml` first, or every rack gets painted twice.

**Already imported the blueprints?** Use **⋮ → Re-import blueprint** on *paint a wine rack* to pick up the dashboard support.
