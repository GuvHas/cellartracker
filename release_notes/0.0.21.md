## A restart during an outage no longer blanks your cellar

Until now, restarting Home Assistant while CellarTracker was unreachable left every sensor unavailable until it came back — although your cellar had not changed.

The last inventory that parsed is now kept on disk and shown in that situation. *Last synchronised* reports when it was saved, not now, so you can see how stale the numbers are, and the next poll comes sooner than your schedule until a live one succeeds.

It is only ever a stand-in for the first refresh. It is never used when CellarTracker rejects your login, so it cannot hide a re-authenticate prompt. And because it holds your purchase history and notes, the file is private, contains no credentials, and is **deleted when you remove the integration**.

## Politer when CellarTracker is struggling

HTTP 429 and 5xx now slow the poll down: the delay doubles with each consecutive failure, capped at six hours, with a little random jitter so installs throttled together do not all return together. It is never sooner than the interval you set, a `Retry-After` from the server is honoured exactly, and your schedule comes back by itself once CellarTracker answers.

A timeout or a refused connection keeps your schedule — those are about the connection, not about load.

## Two actions and an event

None of these create an entity.

| | |
|---|---|
| `cellar_tracker.refresh` | Sync now, instead of at the next poll. Rate-limited by Home Assistant's own debouncer. |
| `cellar_tracker.get_wine_by_bin` | Returns the bottles in a rack bin — name, vintage, drinking window, status. Made for scripts: call it with `response_variable`. |
| `cellartracker_inventory_changed` | Fired when bottles are added or removed, with `added_bottles`, `removed_bottles` and `total_count`. |

The event stays quiet on the first poll after a restart, for a revaluation, and for an edited note. The first live poll after a restart *is* compared with the cached inventory, so bottles changed while Home Assistant was offline are announced.

The lookup leaves out the tasting note, the price paid and the barcode on purpose: service responses land in automation traces, which are shared far more freely than the cellar itself.

Details and examples are in the README, under **Actions and events**.

## Diagnostics you can attach to a bug report

The diagnostics download now also reports whether the cache is being served, the backoff state, why the last poll failed, and the drinking-window counts. It still leaves out your password, your username and your rack names.

## ⚠️ Things you might notice

- **The actions are `cellar_tracker.*`, not `cellartracker.*`.** A service has to be registered under the integration's own domain.
- **`peak` means the middle third of the window.** With `span = EndConsume - BeginConsume`, it runs from `BeginConsume + span/3` to `EndConsume - span/3`, so a wine's first and last years are ready but not peak. It is never true when a window has only one end.
- **Nothing you already have changes.** Entity IDs, the five sensors and the dashboard are as they were.
